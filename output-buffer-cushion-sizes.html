Output buffer — block cells across cushion sizes

The chosen D+B combo (flavor 3), rendered at cushion 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 blocks to check it holds at every size. Capacity is always 2×cushion. The bar is shown at a realistic ~300 px width.

Colour rule: margin cells (1…cushion) are green when full, red when empty (underfill alarm). Headroom cells (cushion+1…2×cushion) are cyan spare, except the top 25% of capacity (min 1 cell) nearest the overflow edge, which are amber. A cyan tick marks the target (= cushion).

So at cushion 2 (capacity 4): target = cell 2; cell 3 = cyan spare; cell 4 = amber. Exactly the question you asked. Each card shows Starved / On-target / Overfull.
