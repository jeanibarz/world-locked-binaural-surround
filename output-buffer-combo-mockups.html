Output buffer — D + B combined

Shared across all three: the first 6 cells are the margin you must keep full — a filled margin cell is green, an empty one glows red (the underfill alarm). A gap + cyan target tick splits the margin from the headroom (spare) cells. The three differ only in how filled headroom reads.

Four states each:  Critically starved (1)  ·  Below margin (4)  ·  On target (6)  ·  Overfull (11).   Cushion = 6 blocks, capacity = 12.
