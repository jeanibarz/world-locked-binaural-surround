Here is why. Plain stereo only controls what is louder in each ear, so sound moves left and right, never in front or behind. In a real room, each ear also hears a sound slightly sooner, and your head and ears add a tonal fingerprint for direction. Capture all of that in one filter, the H R T F, and headphones can place a sound anywhere around you. That is binaural, and it is what lifts sound out of your head.
