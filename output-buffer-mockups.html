Output buffer bar — visualization variants

The output buffer is a setpoint gauge: the FIFO holds up to 2×cushion blocks, the target is cushion blocks (the white tick / center), fill drifting left risks an underrun (starve) and right risks an overrun (drop). Each variant below is shown in three states.

States shown per variant:  Starved (2 / 6 blk)  ·  On target (6 / 6 blk)  ·  Overfull (11 / 6 blk).   Default cushion = 6 blocks, capacity = 12.
