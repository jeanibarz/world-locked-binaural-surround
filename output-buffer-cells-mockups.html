Output buffer — block-cell variants

All build on the #4 block-cells idea (one cell per FIFO block, capacity = 2×cushion). They differ in how they show the target (cushion blocks) and the danger of being below it — which is the state the owner actually sees (the meter rides the 0–1 floor).

Per variant, four states:  Critically starved (1)  ·  Below margin (4)  ·  On target (6)  ·  Overfull (11).   Cushion = 6 blocks, capacity = 12.
